# 💰 Finance Summary Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The Finance Summary Dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, target achievement, team contribution, and salesperson effectiveness. The dashboard enables management to monitor actual sales, compare them against targets, evaluate team performance, and identify areas requiring strategic intervention.

This dashboard helps business leaders make data-driven decisions to improve revenue growth, sales productivity, and target achievement.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Organizations often struggle with:

- Tracking sales performance against targets.
- Identifying underperforming sales teams.
- Monitoring salesperson productivity.
- Measuring target achievement across different periods.
- Understanding revenue trends and variances.
- Making timely business decisions based on sales performance.

The objective of this dashboard is to provide a centralized solution for monitoring sales KPIs and improving overall business performance.

## 📈 Key Business Insights

### Sales Target Achievement

- Total Sales Actual: $18.95M
- Total Sales Target: $19.32M
- Overall performance is below target by $366.86K.

### Team Contribution

- Delish contributes the highest sales.
- Tempo contributes the lowest sales among all teams.

### Salesperson Performance

- Several salespersons exceeded targets.
- Some salespersons are consistently underperforming and require coaching or support.

### Monthly Performance

- Only 2 months successfully achieved sales targets.
- Most months failed to meet planned revenue expectations.

### Variance Analysis

- Overall Variance Percentage: -1.90%
- Indicates a slight shortfall against business goals.

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

### Improve Sales Target Achievement

- Review target-setting strategy.
- Align targets with market demand and historical performance.

### Strengthen Underperforming Teams

- Conduct performance reviews.
- Provide additional sales training and support.

### Incentivize High Performers

- Introduce performance-based rewards.
- Recognize top-performing salespersons.

### Improve Forecast Accuracy

- Use historical sales trends for forecasting.
- Implement predictive analytics for target planning.

### Monitor Performance Regularly

- Weekly and monthly sales reviews.
- Real-time KPI tracking through Power BI dashboards.
